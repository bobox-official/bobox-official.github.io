Bobox Publisher

A private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Bobox to publish his own videos.

This tool uses YouTube API Services

By using Bobox Publisher, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy, our Privacy Policy and our Terms of Use.

What it does

Bobox is a YouTube channel of animated quiz and game videos. The channel owner makes every video on his own computer, then uses Bobox Publisher, a command-line tool that runs only on that computer, to:

⬆️

Upload

Uploads the finished videos to his own channel as private, with a scheduled publication date.

✏️

Video details

Sets titles, descriptions, tags, translations, audience ("not made for kids") and the altered or synthetic content disclosure.

🖼️

Thumbnails and playlists

Sets custom thumbnails and organizes the channel's playlists.

📊

Channel statistics

Reads the channel's own statistics to plan the next videos.

Not a public service. Bobox Publisher is not distributed, sold or offered to anyone. It has a single user, the channel owner, who signs in with his own Google account through Google's standard OAuth screen. It only acts on his own channel.
